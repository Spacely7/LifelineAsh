What We Offer

Our Services

A comprehensive suite of healthcare services delivered by a multidisciplinary team of qualified professionals — onsite, on time, and beyond expectation.

🩺

Pre-enrollment Medicals

Comprehensive Entry Health Assessment

Our pre-enrollment medical service is designed to give organizations a clear picture of the health status of new members, employees, or students before they officially join. This ensures that any existing conditions are identified early and managed appropriately.

The service includes detailed history taking, physical examinations, generation of medical reports, professional health advice, and referrals to specialists where necessary.

History Taking Medical Reports Health Advice Specialist Referrals Corporates Schools
🏢

Onsite Medical Check-up

Healthcare Delivered to Your Door

We bring our medical team directly to your workplace, school, or premises to conduct thorough medical assessments. This eliminates the need for employees or members to leave your premises, saving time and increasing participation.

Each onsite visit includes a full health assessment, examination, and where required, the issuance of an official medical certificate by our licensed physicians.

Full Assessment Medical Certificates Onsite Delivery All Sectors
📋

Health Policy Development

Building a Healthier Organization

A sound health policy is the foundation of a healthy workplace. Our team works with organizational leadership to develop, review, and implement comprehensive health policies tailored to your specific sector and workforce.

We ensure your policy is legally compliant, practical to implement, and designed to genuinely improve health outcomes for your people.

Policy Review Implementation Compliance Corporates Schools
🔬

Screening Programs

Early Detection Saves Lives

Our health screening programs are designed to detect conditions before they become serious. From blood pressure and diabetes screening to vision checks and more, we provide targeted programs based on your organization's risk profile.

Early detection allows for timely intervention, reducing the long-term health and financial impact of unmanaged conditions on your workforce and community.

Blood Pressure Diabetes Vision BMI All Ages
💉

Vaccination & Immunization

Protecting Your Community

Lifeline Haven provides both child immunization programs and adult vaccination services. Our pharmacists and nurses ensure safe, accurate administration of vaccines in a comfortable, private setting.

We can coordinate mass vaccination drives for organizations, schools, and communities, handling logistics, record-keeping, and follow-up reminders for booster doses.

Child Immunization Adult Vaccination Mass Drives Record Keeping
🎓

Health Education & Training

Knowledge Is the Best Medicine

Our health education and training sessions are interactive, evidence-based, and tailored to your audience — whether that's a corporate boardroom, a school classroom, or a community hall. Topics include hygiene, nutrition, disease prevention, mental health, environmental safety, and first aid.

We also offer certified training programs for health and safety officers within organizations.

Hygiene Nutrition Mental Health First Aid H&S Training
🧠

Psychosocial & Wellness

Mind, Body & Community Wellness

Mental and emotional health is as important as physical wellbeing. Our clinical psychologists and counselors offer psychosocial support, group wellness activities, and one-on-one counselling sessions for individuals and teams.

Our wellness programs are designed to boost morale, reduce stress, and create a positive, productive atmosphere in any organization.

Counselling Stress Management Group Sessions Wellness Activities
🌿

Occupational Health & Safety

A Safe Environment for Everyone

Our occupational health and environmental management services help organizations identify hazards, assess risks, and implement systems to protect workers and the surrounding environment. This covers physical, chemical, biological, and ergonomic risks.

We produce detailed reports, recommend control measures, and work with your team to ensure compliance with Ghana's occupational health and safety regulations.

Risk Assessment Hazard Identification Compliance Environmental Mgmt Industrial
